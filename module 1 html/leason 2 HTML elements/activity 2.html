Albert Einstein was born on March 14, 1879, in Ulm, Württemberg, Germany, to secular Ashkenazi Jewish parents, Hermann Einstein and Pauline Koch Wikipedia Wikipedia +1 . He had one sister, Maria (Maja), and showed an early fascination with science and mathematics. At age five, a compass sparked his curiosity about invisible forces, and by age 12, he was deeply engaged with geometry, calling a book of geometry his “sacred little geometry book” Encyclopedia Britannica Encyclopedia Britannica +1 . Einstein studied at the Luitpold Gymnasium in Munich and later at Aarau, Switzerland, before enrolling in the Swiss Federal Polytechnic School in Zurich in 1896 to train as a teacher in physics and mathematics
