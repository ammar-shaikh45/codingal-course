Morning Fitness Routine

Welcome! Follow this simple 3-step workout every morning to boost your energy and stay active.

1. Warm-Up

Duration: 5 Minutes

Light jogging in place, arm circles, and gentle neck stretches to prepare your muscles.

2. Strength Training

Duration: 15 Minutes

3 sets of 12 push-ups, 15 bodyweight squats, and a 30-second plank hold.

3. Cool Down

Duration: 5 Minutes

Deep breathing exercises and full-body static stretches to improve flexibility.

Fitness Tip: Stay hydrated! Drink a full glass of water before and after your workout.
