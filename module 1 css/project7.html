Featured Attractions

1. Sky-High Zip Line

Soar 150 feet above the forest canopy at speeds up to 45 mph. Experience breathtaking panoramic views while secured in a full-body safety harness.

Minimum height requirement: 48 inches

2. Emerald Rope Course

Navigate swinging bridges, tightropes, and cargo nets. Every obstacle requires focus, balance, and agility to complete before the timer runs out.

Difficulty level: Intermediate to Pro

3. Summit Climbing Wall

Scale our 60-ft artificial granite cliff. Ring the victory bell at the top to earn your official Summit Conqueror badge!

Auto-belay safety systems included

Park Safety Guidelines

Follow these essential rules to keep every adventurer safe:

  • Wear closed-toe shoes at all times on courses.
  • Keep your helmet buckled until you exit the landing zone.
  • Listen carefully to your certified trail guide.
  • Stay hydrated—refill stations are located at every base camp.
